Our Mission

We empower local talent while transforming environmental impact. At REEVA, we connect donors, emerging designers, and conscious consumers to give every garment a second life.

Our Values

REEVA is a movement driven by sustainability, innovation, and inclusion. Our mission is to transform the environmental impact of the textile industry through the circular economy.

Sustainability

We reduce environmental impact by connecting donors with conscious buyers, giving every garment a second life.

Innovation

We boost local talent through upcycling techniques that turn textile materials into unique, high-quality pieces.

Inclusion

We foster a community committed to the planet, connecting people who donate with conscious buyers.

Circular Economy

We drive a business model that prioritizes reuse and the circular economy for a more sustainable future.
